# Print sheet: strain mandrels

Generated by cmtool at commit `8163c375b3269ba671e0325e0b9c07a855c3055f-dirty` on 2026-09-20.

## Purpose

Measure the allowable bending strain. Wrap a test strip around each post and look for whitening, cracking and permanent set -- first on a single bend, then after ten cycles. The strain a post imposes is (t/2)/(R + t/2), so the posts cover roughly 0.008 to 0.057 strain across the three strip thicknesses. The largest strain that still passes after ten cycles sets `allowable_strain`, which is the number that decides how far every flexure in the project may bend.

## Slicer settings (Bambu Studio)

Design choices for printing thin flexures consistently, not measurements.
Verify the exact setting names in your slicer version.

| Setting | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wall_generator | `Arachne` | Arachne varies extrusion width to fill thin features exactly. The classic generator works in whole-nozzle-width perimeters and will thin, distort or drop a sub-2-perimeter wall -- exactly the 0.4-0.6 mm flexures this project depends on. This is the single most important setting here. |
| wall_loops | `4` | Enough perimeters that rigid links are genuinely solid; flexures are thinner than this and become a single variable-width wall. |
| sparse_infill_density_percent | `100` | Rigid links must be rigid, and density must be uniform for the modulus measured on a coupon to apply to a mechanism. Sparse infill makes 'rigid' links compliant and introduces a second unmeasured variable. |
| detect_thin_wall | `True` | Prevents narrow regions being skipped entirely. |
| seam_position | `aligned, placed away from flexures` | A layer seam on a flexure is a stress concentrator and a crack initiation site. |
| outer_wall_speed_mm_per_s | `30` | Slower outer walls improve dimensional accuracy on thin features. Reduce further if 0.4-0.5 mm strips come out inconsistent. |
| nozzle_temperature_c | _to be set_ | PLACEHOLDER: use the filament vendor's recommendation for the actual spool, and log it in the print metadata. |
| bed_temperature_c | _to be set_ | PLACEHOLDER: as above. |
| supports | `False` | Parts are designed flat with the mechanism plane on the bed; support material on a flexure would ruin its surface. |

## How to run this test

1. Print the mandrel block and the strips together, from the same spool.
2. Measure each strip's thickness with calipers before bending anything: the strain depends on the as-printed thickness, not the nominal one.
3. Start at the largest post (lowest strain) and work down.
4. Wrap a strip about 90 degrees around the post, hold for five seconds, release.
5. Record whitening, any crack, and the permanent set angle after release.
6. Repeat the same strip and post ten times, then record the same three things.
7. Mark each strip and post combination pass, marginal or fail in the CSV.
8. The largest nominal strain that still passes after ten cycles, across every repeat, is the allowable strain. Put it in configs/materials/pla.yaml with status: measured and the date.
9. Fill in strain_test_template.csv as you go; it already has the nominal strains.
